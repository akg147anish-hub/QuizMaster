👑 HOW TO PLAY QUIZMASTER

1
The 15 Mixed Question Gauntlet

Every match presents 15 randomized questions spanning General Knowledge, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Sports, Science, History & Geography, Entertainment, and Technology.

2
Progressive Difficulty & XP Rewards

Questions advance as you climb: Q1-3 (Easy, 10 XP), Q4-6 (Medium, 20 XP), Q7-9 (Hard, 30 XP), Q10-12 (Very Hard, 40 XP), Q13-15 (Extreme, 50 XP). A 100 XP Victory bonus rewards flawless completion!

3
45-Second Timer per Question

You have 45 seconds to confirm your answer. Warnings trigger at 15 seconds, and critical pulses at 5 seconds. If the timer hits zero, the match is lost!

4
The 3 Strategic Lifelines
  • ✂️ 50:50: Vanishes two incorrect options, leaving the correct one and one rival.
  • 💡 Brain Hint: Reveals a thoughtful clue to guide your deduction without spoiling the answer.
  • 🛡️ Second Chance: Protects you from one incorrect guess. If your first answer is wrong, the shield triggers and lets you pick again!
5
Locking Your Answer

Select your desired option and press the golden "Lock Answer" button. A single wrong answer (without Second Chance) ends the match.
